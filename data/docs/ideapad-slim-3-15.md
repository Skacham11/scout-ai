# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 15

Source: https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/c/laptops/ideapad/
Category: product notes

## Config in catalog

Around $549 in this snapshot: Ryzen 5 7520U, 8GB RAM, 256GB storage, 15.6-inch 1080p, about 3.54 lb.

## Strengths

Cheap. Large screen for notes and video. Fine for docs, browsing, and light coursework.

## Weaknesses

8GB RAM is a poor fit for Docker, multiple IDE windows, and modern browsers. 256GB storage fills up quickly. Not a professional development machine.

## Fit

Budget student laptop only. Do not recommend as a primary SWE laptop if the budget can reach the $900 band.
