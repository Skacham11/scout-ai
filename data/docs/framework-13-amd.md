# Framework Laptop 13 (AMD Ryzen AI 300)

Source: https://frame.work/laptop13
Category: product notes

## Config in catalog

Around $1,399 in this snapshot: Ryzen AI 9 365 class CPU, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD, 13.5-inch high-res display, about 2.87 lb.

## Strengths

Repairable and upgradeable. Expansion cards let you choose ports. Good for developers who want to keep a machine for years. 32GB RAM available without jumping to a giant chassis.

## Weaknesses

More DIY than Apple or Dell. Build and support feel different from a traditional OEM. Not aimed at gaming.

## Fit

Best when upgradeability and repair matter. A bit more setup mindset than a sealed ultrabook.
