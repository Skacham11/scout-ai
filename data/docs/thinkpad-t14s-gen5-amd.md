# ThinkPad T14s Gen 5 (AMD)

Source: https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/p/laptops/thinkpad/thinkpadt/thinkpad-t14s-gen-5-14-inch-amd-windows-laptop/len101t0075
Category: product notes

## Config in catalog

Around $1,549 in this snapshot: Ryzen 7 8840U, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD, 14-inch 1920x1200, about 3.17 lb.

## Strengths

ThinkPad keyboard. 32GB RAM helps with Docker and IDEs. Linux-friendly AMD platform. More practical ports than many ultraportables, including HDMI and USB-A on typical configs.

## Weaknesses

Heavier and pricier than a MacBook Air. Battery is solid but not class-leading. Business styling, not a gaming machine.

## Fit

Strong primary coding laptop on Windows or Linux when keyboard quality and RAM matter more than lowest weight.
