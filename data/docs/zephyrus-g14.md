# ASUS ROG Zephyrus G14

Source: https://rog.asus.com/laptops/rog-zephyrus/rog-zephyrus-g14-2024/
Category: product notes

## Config in catalog

Around $1,599 in this snapshot: Ryzen 9 8945HS, 16GB RAM, 1TB SSD, RTX 4060, 14-inch 2560x1600 165Hz, about 3.3 lb.

## Strengths

Discrete GPU for occasional gaming and heavier local compute. Still a 14-inch form factor. Higher refresh display.

## Weaknesses

Stock 16GB RAM is light for a "coding plus gaming" machine and should often be upgraded. Louder fans under load. Worse battery life than ultrabooks when the dGPU wakes up.

## Fit

Pick this when you need a GPU. If you only write code and travel, a lighter non-gaming laptop is usually better.
